Open FactoryOps AI support

Before reporting a problem

  • Include the module, browser/device and steps to reproduce.
  • Include a screenshot only after removing personal, customer and confidential factory information.
  • Never include passwords, access tokens, Supabase keys, employee data or production-sensitive records.
  • For cloud issues, identify whether the problem is authentication, company membership, record synchronization or a database policy error. Do not post private project credentials.

FactoryOps AI is currently an evolving MVP. The public issue tracker is not a guaranteed emergency or service-level support channel.
